SYNTHETIC - FICTIONAL LENDERS, HYPOTHETICAL RATE EVENT

A competitor cut its fee. Should we copy it?

PLN 8,000 / 12 months, new customers. Both options are compared in the same month, so the rate cut affects them equally.

1. What happened

Hypothetical NBP cut: legal interest cap falls to 14.00%. Fee cap stays PLN 1,600.00.

Competitor X (fictional): Month 1 fee PLN 1,400.00 + interest PLN 642.16 at 14.5%; Month 2 fee PLN 1,250.00 + interest PLN 619.56 at 14.0%.

3. Break-even

Copying pays off only if take-up rises from 35% to 40.7%. Business question: will a PLN 150.00 lower fee bring that many more customers?

4. Stress tests

  • Extra customers are riskier (PD 15% instead of 10%): copy vs keep = PLN -31,513.20.
  • Keeping our fee loses customers (take-up 33%): copy vs keep = PLN -3,272.00.

5. Decision

Do not copy automatically. Copying pays off only if take-up reaches the break-even rate; test it in a controlled experiment and check the risk of the extra customers.

Not modelled

  • Early repayment fee refunds (Art. 49) - reduce fee income in both options.
  • Repeat loans - more new customers may pay off later; the strongest argument for copying.
  • Discounting, capital and tax.
