# Methodology, sources, and disclosure boundaries

**Frozen research date:** 31 October 2026.

## Observational scope

- **SPL:** selected public paid examples for PLN 2,000/30 days; 11 observations, PLN 239.83–240.23 in the archived comparison.
- **INST:** selected public examples for PLN 8,000/12 months; 4 observations, PLN 1,159–2,236.07.
- 15/18 is **scenario coverage within a fixed roster**, not lender-market share and not automatically approved offer coverage.
- Customer segments/eligibility/optional services are not fully standardized across lenders.

## Ceiling interpretation

The PLN 240.27 SPL reference is a scenario-based calculation using then-applicable maximum nominal interest and non-interest cost assumptions for a 30-day lump-sum loan. The PLN 2,242.16 INST reference assumes a 12-instalment annuity with a then-applicable maximum nominal rate plus a statutory non-interest charge reference. Neither is a universal borrower quote or invariant legal maximum for all loan structures.

**Important:** The observed close clustering supports the conclusion that the ceiling is a powerful pricing constraint. It does not independently prove each lender deliberately set its maximum permitted price, nor rule out price competition in other customer segments.

## Source integrity and public release

The local archive may include screenshots, page text, cached HTML and evidence hashes. Hashes allow internal checking for accidental alterations; they do not demonstrate that a lender approved a quote. Before publishing a sample, ensure screenshots/text may be distributed and do not contain credentials, personal data, tracking IDs or sensitive customer details. If rights or provenance are uncertain, publish derived aggregate metrics and synthetic fixtures instead.
