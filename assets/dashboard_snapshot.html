Short-term loans · PLN 2,000 / 30 days

11 of 11 paid offers price near the ceiling

Prices range from PLN 239.83 to PLN 240.23, just PLN 0.04–0.44 below the PLN 240.27 statutory reference. The law, not demonstrated price imitation, explains the clustering. First-loan-free offers, loan limits and payout speed are the competitive dimensions to test.

Instalment loans · PLN 8,000 / 12 months

Provident is the price outlier

At PLN 1,159, its quoted cost is far below the other three (PLN 2,000–2,236). Hoprata’s PLN 2,236.07 is PLN 6.09 below the modelled PLN 2,242.16 scenario ceiling. Eligibility and fee structure still need matching.

How the ceilings work: Short-term reference = PLN 216.44 maximum non-interest cost under Art. 36a + PLN 23.84 interest at the 14.5% annual contractual ceiling. Instalment reference = PLN 1,600 maximum non-interest cost + about PLN 642.16 interest for 12 equal monthly repayments on PLN 8,000. The latter is a modelled annuity benchmark, not a flat cap applicable to every fee or repayment structure. Public prices have different borrower eligibility and are not approved quotes.

New vs returning customers: where competition moves

Selected official examples - terms and eligibility differ
New customers

Acquire with a conditional 0% first loan

At the statutory paid-price ceiling, first-loan promotions offer another lever: the free-loan amount, 30- or 61-day term, approval experience and payment speed. A 61-day free loan is not comparable with a 30-day paid quote.

Returning customers

Earn contribution only if customers come back

Paid repeat lending can potentially recover acquisition cost, funding, servicing and expected credit losses. Public rate tables do not prove that return rates or cohort economics are positive.

Strategic question: Which first-loan offer generates the highest risk-adjusted 180-day customer contribution - not simply the most new applications?

The acquisition economics to investigate
1. Attract

0% eligibility, first-loan limit, promotion term, traffic and acquisition cost

2. Convert & repay

Approval rate, disbursement speed, on-time repayment and first-loan credit losses

3. Retain

Repeat-borrowing rate, time to second loan, paid margin, servicing cost and responsible-lending controls

Measure net 180-day cohort contribution by acquisition campaign and initial term. Longer free loans may increase funding exposure; economics depend on observed repayment, risk and retention, which are not in this public dataset.

Executive recommendation

The statutory ceiling - not evidence of copycat pricing - explains why standard paid short-term loan costs cluster. Test acquisition offers, loan limits and decision speed as potential competitive levers. Resolve InstaFin eligibility and explain Provident’s lower observed cost before recommending commercial changes.

The synthetic positive-path test demonstrates workflow behaviour, not a real lender repricing.
